\section{Computational evidence}\label{sec:computational-evidence}

The exact characterization is proved analytically.  We additionally include a finite arithmetic audit to make the threshold structure and known-size residual gap directly inspectable.  The existing script \texttt{audit/audit\_formula.py} checks theorem corollaries and split inequalities; the companion \texttt{audit/computational\_evidence.py} exports a wider finite table.

For the known-diameter theorem, the exact formula gives the following representative values.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{c|rrrr}
$D=8$ & $P=1$ & $P=2$ & $P=4$ & $P=8$ \\
\hline
$S_D(D,P)$ & 10 & 6 & 4 & 3
\end{tabular}
\caption{Finite evaluation of $S_D(D,P)=\lceil D/P\rceil+2$ at $D=8$.  The three-state threshold occurs exactly at $P=D$.}
\label{tab:two-orbit-finite}
\end{table}

Across the audited grid the script verifies both $P(S_D-2)\ge D$ and the minimality condition $P(S_D-3)<D$ whenever $S_D>3$.  It separately records the known-size lower/upper interval
\[
2+\left\lceil\frac{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}{P}\right\rceil
\le S_n(n,P)\le
2+\left\lceil\frac{n-1}{P}\right\rceil,
\]
so the remaining finite gap is visible rather than hidden by asymptotic notation.  These checks are computational evidence only and are not used in the proof of the exact known-diameter result.
